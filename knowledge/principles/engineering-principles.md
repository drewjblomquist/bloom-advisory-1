# Engineering principles for Bloom Advisory

> Generated synthesis inputs from the central knowledge base. These are traceable recommendations, not absolute requirements.

- Many AI roadmap engagements by consultancies follow a predictable sequence: executive/top-level interviews, a survey, a data request, follow-up interviews, a recommendations deck with ROI estimates built on assumptions, and a final presentation (plus invoice). _(author claim; [Anecdote: consultancies' AI roadmaps often end with a recommendations deck + invoice](https://x.com/mardehaym/status/2095963557150224492))_
- Recommendations decks commonly contain ROI estimates that rely on explicit or implicit assumptions rather than validated proofs of value. _(author claim; [Anecdote: consultancies' AI roadmaps often end with a recommendations deck + invoice](https://x.com/mardehaym/status/2095963557150224492))_
- Consultancy engagements often deliver strategic artifacts (decks) rather than implemented systems; the engagement can end once the deliverable and invoice are issued. _(author claim; [Anecdote: consultancies' AI roadmaps often end with a recommendations deck + invoice](https://x.com/mardehaym/status/2095963557150224492))_
